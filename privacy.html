Business Desk — Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Business Desk is a private work tool used by its owner only. This page explains what information the tool handles and why.

Sign in with Google

The Desk offers "Sign in with Google" so the owner can label each workspace with one of his own Google accounts. When the owner signs in, Google shares the account's name and email address with the Desk. The Desk uses them only to:

  • show which Google account each workspace belongs to, and
  • attach that account label to messages the owner sends from that workspace to his own private back end.

The account labels and workspace choices are stored on the owner's own device (in the browser's local storage). The Desk never sees or stores Google passwords.

What the Desk does not do

  • It does not read, send, or delete email.
  • It does not access Google Drive, contacts, calendars, or any other Google service.
  • It does not sell or share account information with advertisers or third parties.
  • It does not show images of people.

Messages

Messages the owner sends from the Desk go to the owner's own private back-end service and are used only so the owner and his assistant can work together on his projects. They are not used for advertising or shared with third parties.
